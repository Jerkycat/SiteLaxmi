# Prologue — What doth life?

We are born, we live, and in the end, we die. Death is a natural part of our life. It's the end, and the end comes for everyone, but much sooner for some than others. It’s hard to deal with the death of someone close to you, be it someone from your family, friend or even a neighbor you would usually greet, specially when death comes so fast that it would be impossible to imagine that person who was there, out of nowhere, disappears from your life, and will never 

ever 

be back…

I’ve always been shy all my life, I was never much fond of people, be it because socializing was hard for me or there wasn't much interesting things I could say to someone. But I liked spending time with my family, I lived in a family of 5, both of my  parents were hard working people who would spending many and many daily hours at the office, so they didn't have much time to our family, but when they did, we would enjoy the moment as a family. 

While they were out working, the one who would take care of my brother Daisuke (who was 4 years old at the time), and me (who was 12), would be my sister Chiaki (who was 16, Chiaki was a very annoying sister who would like to pick on me, but even though she had that nature, she was very but very intelligent and would spend many and many times studying to be able to enter an university, and while Chiaki would study, she would leave me to take care of Daisuke, Daisuke was a very special type of boy, his smile and kindness was always overflowing, he would always ask me to play some sport with him, the reason our parents worked so much was because they wanted to give us a bright future as we were very far from being rich, and all of his children indeed did have a bright future, except one.

Chiaki became a well succeeded cardiologist, I was told that she might be the very best on that area.

Daisuke is only 16 but he already is a star at young baseball, the very best batter.

I was became an unsuccessful beta tester.

I guess I always liked games since I know myself as a person, my favorite game was and always will be the Kōuken series (鋼拳), it's the best fighting game ever featuring bizarre characters and a lot of violence, the playhub 1 and 2, together with ALL of the 
KŌUKEN GAMES, 1, 2, 3, tag, 4 and 5! I loved playing and playing against the CPUs, my favorite character was called Kazuma, a young man in seek of power and revenge, using the special martial arts that were taught to him by his family, my sister and my brother would sometimes play with me, but Daisuke didn't have much interest in games, and Chiaki didn't have much time to play due to studies, even though she really enjoyed it, and was better than me...

	Things changed in the first year of high school, where I made a friend, just like me, he was fascinated by games, but unlike me, he was able to interact with the others, even though he looked worse than I did, wearing a shirt with a picture of his waifu on it, messy hair, a big belly and a strange smell of potato chips, he didn't feel embarrassed to reach anyone and ask about anything, and I could barely speak to the cashier in the convenience store near my house.

 One day he took me to a tournament of Kōuken, I was very anxious because there were so many people.

"Hideyoshi, let's go home."

"Oh, don't be afraid Makoto, it's going to be a lot of fun, we'll go there quietly, win, and leave."

We started playing, and in the end I actually won the tournament, Hideyoshi was in 3rd place, he lost to a guy that was very scary and he played Rei Hashi, Kazuma’s father and enemy, the battle was so intense that the moment I won, I just couldn’t believe, I was so happy to hold the trophy in my hands with Hideyoshi by my side, if he hadn't dragged me there, I'd never have had the chance to play, this event solidified our friendship like nothing else. 

We used to go to each other's houses to play video games, read manga or watch anime, we managed to get into the same college and study video game programming & design and achieve the dream of living testing new games that haven't been released yet, in this job we would receive games from companies, try to find glitches and give objective opinions on how they could improve the game, but as most of the companies are close to Tokyo, it would be better if we moved there. Before I left home, I gave my family a big hug, even though they didn't pay much attention to me, they were the ones who looked after me, took me in, fed me and gave me a home. With a fallen tear, I promised that I would be back one day.

	Our life in Tokyo was good, we made a living testing unreleased games. One day, while we were playing the brand new game Kōuken game for the playhub 3, Hideyoshi asked me:

“Makoto, Have you ever thought of doing something besides playing video games, watching anime, eating and sleeping?”

“What do you mean? Wasn't this the life we always wanted to have?”

“Yeah haha, you are right! But like my father told me once, ‘the cycle of a man's life is to be born, have children and die’, I don’t know if I would feel ‘complete’ without having any children or at least had a love in life.”

“I think you are playtesting too many VNs, but who would ever want us? Hahaha…”

“For you it’s easy, I don’t think you ever realized but Masako from 11th grade was deeply in love with you, you were always popular between the girls, as for me, I was the butt of jokes, hahahaha.”

“...”

“But it's a really good question, who would ever want me? I’ve met so many people in my life, yet you are the only one who chose to be truly my friend until now.”

“You are my only friend too… If you need anything, I will support you.”

“Thank you Makoto, I will find a way to have a girlfriend… I want a girl that loves me unconditionally one day!”

“...”

“NOW TAKE THIS!”

“Whaaaat?? Why does your back right kick has so much range? This is so fucking stupid”

“The Emperor cannot be beaten, all hail The Emperor and his Wrestling! Roar!”

“You laugh now, but I am playing Kazuma, the poster boy of this game, and he won’t be beat by a weirdo with leopard head!”



	Hideyoshi Haraguchi was my friend since the first day I met him, he was my friend for 8 years. He fell victim to an accident last week, hit by a truck while crossing the road to the convenience store. When they told me about what happened, I was in shock. It didn't seem real at first, I felt normal, but gradually it sunk in. There were very few people at his funeral for someone so energetic. It was sad, in a way that becomes almost mundane, as if he were just one more.

	Gradually, I found myself in exile. I didn't cry, but I felt sadness, an emptiness in my heart, and no strength to do anything, mentally or physically. I tried playing video games to distract myself, but while playing, it only reminded me of my friend who had always been by my side. The apartment remains surrounded by anime and game posters he was obsessed with. I haven't left the house since the funeral. I barely get out of bed, I spend most of my time sleeping or lying in bed, staring at the ceiling of the dark room.

“Why did he have to die?”

The more I asked myself that question, the more I felt like I was drowning, sinking into a dark ocean, until at some point, I was completely surrounded by darkness and simply vanished from this world… That was, at least, what I expected…
